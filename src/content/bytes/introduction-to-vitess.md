**Presented at the first [KubeCon + CloudNativeCon India](https://kccncind2024.sched.com/event/1mpDb/introduction-to-vitess-scalable-database-for-modern-applications-manan-gupta-harshit-gangal-planetscale) in Delhi, with Harshit Gangal.** The full title is *Introduction to Vitess: Scalable Database for Modern Applications*.

This is the Vitess introductory session. Harshit and I cover what Vitess is, the vocabulary you need to follow its architecture, and the features it offers. Then we demo how Vitess serves query traffic as a distributed database. We close with what landed in recent releases and what was coming in the next one.
